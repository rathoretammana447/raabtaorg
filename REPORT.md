# Rabta — Organization Profile

## Organization identity
- **Name:** Rabta (राबता)
- **Tagline:** Education. Skills. Opportunity. Empowerment.
- **Founder:** Tammana Rathore — Founder & Educator
- **Location:** Derabassi, Punjab, India
- **Current reach:** Approximately 10–15 children
- **Nature:** Community-based educational initiative / NGO, as described in the source profile

## About
Rabta is a grassroots educational initiative founded on the belief that a child's access to education should not be limited by financial circumstances. It provides free educational support, guidance, and practical life-skills training to underprivileged children.

## Vision
> “A future where every child has access to education, skills and the opportunity to build a better life.”

## Mission areas
- Free educational support and stronger academic foundations
- English communication and confidence
- Practical and life-oriented skills
- Career and employment awareness
- Self-reliance and financial independence
- A supportive environment where children can learn without fear

## Programmes
### Academic education
English, science, social sciences, general knowledge, basic computer/digital literacy, homework, and examination preparation.

### Communication skills
Confident speaking, English communication, self-introductions, conversations, interview practice, and public-speaking confidence.

### Career and employment readiness
Job interviews, CV/resume preparation, professional communication, workplace behaviour, interview etiquette, career awareness, and job opportunities.

### Life skills
Decision-making, problem-solving, time management, personal responsibility, financial awareness, confidence, teamwork, and leadership.

## Current reach and approach
The source profile reports approximately 10–15 students. The founder personally teaches and mentors students in a community-based setting.

## Student employment-readiness example
The source profile describes one student preparing for an interview opportunity with Starbucks. It does not state that the student has secured the job.

## Core values
Empathy, education, empowerment, growth, opportunity, and dignity.

## Five-step approach
1. **Identify** — Understand educational and developmental needs.
2. **Educate** — Provide free academic support.
3. **Develop** — Build communication, digital, and life skills.
4. **Guide** — Provide career and employment guidance.
5. **Empower** — Help students become confident and self-reliant.

## Future direction
**Short term:** Increase student reach; introduce structured learning; expand English and computer education; run career-awareness and interview-preparation sessions.

**Medium term:** Build a volunteer educator network; create structured skill programmes; establish partnerships; organize career workshops; expand food-support initiatives.

**Long term:** Develop a scalable community platform connecting children, education, skills, mentorship, and opportunities.
